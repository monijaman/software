## Use a few sites well

Pick 3–4 sites that match your target and check them daily with saved alerts. Spreading thin across 20 sites wastes time.

## General job boards

| Site | Best for | Tip |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **LinkedIn Jobs** | Almost every role, local and global | Set alerts by title + location; apply in the first 48 hours |
| **Indeed** | Large volume, many countries | Use exact title searches and filters |
| **Glassdoor** | Jobs + company reviews + salaries | Read interview reviews before your rounds |
| **Company career pages** | Roles that never reach job boards | Bookmark your top 20 companies |

## Bangladesh

| Site | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Bdjobs.com** | Largest local board; many IT and software roles |
| **LinkedIn (Bangladesh filter)** | Most product companies and foreign-funded startups post here |
| **Skill.jobs** | Local jobs plus training programs |
| **Company career pages** | Many software companies hire directly; follow their pages |
| **Developer communities** | Facebook/Discord tech groups often share referrals and openings; verify every post |

## Remote jobs (work for a foreign company)

| Site | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **We Work Remotely** | One of the oldest remote-only boards |
| **Remote OK** | Remote tech jobs, filter by stack |
| **Remotive** | Curated remote jobs, weekly newsletter |
| **Himalayas** | Remote jobs with salary and time-zone filters |
| **Arc.dev / Turing** | Vetted remote developer platforms with a test process |
| **Wellfound (formerly AngelList Talent)** | Startups, shows salary and equity upfront |
| **Y Combinator – Work at a Startup** | Jobs at YC-backed startups |
| **Hacker News "Who is hiring?"** | Monthly thread, posted the first weekday of each month |
| **Welcome to the Jungle** | Startup and tech company jobs (absorbed Otta) |

> 💡 For remote roles, state your time-zone overlap in your resume summary, for example "Available 4 hours overlap with EU/US East".

## Freelance and contract

| Site | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Upwork** | Hourly and fixed-price projects; strong profile and niche matter |
| **Fiverr** | Package your service as a fixed "gig" |
| **Toptal** | Selective, higher rates, tough screening |
| **Contra** | Commission-free freelance profiles |

## Salary research

- **levels.fyi**: salaries by company and level (strongest for big tech).
- **Glassdoor**: salaries and reviews.
- Ask peers. A short anonymous range from 2–3 people is very useful.

## Avoid scams

Red flags: asks you to **pay** for training or equipment, interview only on chat apps, offer without any interview, vague company with no website or LinkedIn presence, asks for bank details early. Real employers never charge you to get a job.
