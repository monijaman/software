## Research first

Before any salary talk, know a range for **this role, level and city / remote market**: levels.fyi, Glassdoor, recent job posts with salary, and 2–3 peers.

## "What is your expected salary?"

- Early on, you can ask: *"Could you share the budgeted range for this role?"*
- If you must give a number, give a **range** whose bottom is a number you would happily accept: *"Based on my research for this level, I'm looking at 1.6–1.9 lakh BDT per month, depending on the full package."*

## Compare the whole offer

| Item | Questions to ask |
| --- | --- |
| Base salary | Monthly or yearly? Gross or net? |
| Bonus | Guaranteed or performance-based? How many per year? |
| Increments | When is the first review? Typical percent? |
| Benefits | Health insurance, provident fund, gratuity, lunch, transport |
| Remote / hybrid | Days in office, equipment, internet allowance |
| Learning | Training budget, certification fees |
| Equity | Number of options, vesting schedule, company stage |
| Leave | Annual, sick, public holidays |
| Notice period and probation | Length and conditions |

## How to negotiate

1. **Thank them** and show excitement first.
2. Ask for time: 2–3 days is normal.
3. Make one clear, reasoned request:

```text
Thank you for the offer, I'm excited about the role and the team.
Based on my research and my experience running payment systems in
production, I was hoping for a base closer to 1.8 lakh. If we can
reach that, I'm ready to accept this week.
```

4. If salary is fixed, ask for something else: joining bonus, earlier review, remote days, training budget.
5. Get the final offer **in writing** before resigning from your current job.

## Things to avoid

- Lying about another offer.
- Accepting and then continuing to interview elsewhere.
- Negotiating on many small items one at a time. Bundle your requests.
